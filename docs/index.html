In Mail it reads the thread. Switch on Discord, WhatsApp, or any app you pick, and the same ⌥H screenshots the window instead, sends it to your model as the context, and puts the finished message into the chat box. It never presses Send. You do.

Example thread and chats, so you can see the mechanism. The panel matches v0.12.

  1. Switch on the chat apps. Mail needs no setup. For the rest: Settings → General → Compose from a Screenshot. Discord and WhatsApp are one toggle each. Add App… picks anything else.
  2. Press ⌥H in the chat. The plugin screenshots the front window, shows you the thumbnail it will send, and you type what you want to say.
  3. Read it, then send it yourself. Insert puts the message into the chat box, unsent. Press Enter when it says what you mean.

Needs a model that can read images (current Claude, GPT, and Gemini models all do), plus two macOS permissions: Screen Recording to take the screenshot and Accessibility to type into the chat box. Without Screen Recording it writes from your notes alone. Without Accessibility it copies the message to your clipboard. Both stay off until you switch on your first app, so Mail-only use never asks for them.

Three principles

Built to stay out of your way.

01

Reads the whole conversation

In Mail, the AI gets the full email thread as context. It sees what's been said, who said it, and what still needs a response. In Discord or WhatsApp it reads a screenshot of the window instead.

02

Use any model you want

Claude, GPT, Gemini, anything on OpenRouter, and TrustedTokens (EU-sovereign) — or a model running on your own Mac through Ollama or LM Studio. The plugin fetches each provider's live model list, so new releases show up the day they ship.

03

Your keys. Your data.

No accounts, no subscriptions, no servers in the middle. API keys live in the macOS Keychain, and your email goes only to the provider you picked — or nowhere at all, if you run a local model.
