Three principles

Built to stay out of your way.

01

Reads the whole conversation

The AI gets the full email thread as context — not just the last message. It sees what's been said, who said it, and what still needs a response.

02

Use any model you want

Claude, GPT, Gemini, anything on OpenRouter — or a model running on your own Mac through Ollama or LM Studio. The plugin fetches each provider's live model list, so new releases show up the day they ship.

03

Your keys. Your data.

No accounts, no subscriptions, no servers in the middle. API keys live in the macOS Keychain, and your email goes only to the provider you picked — or nowhere at all, if you run a local model.
